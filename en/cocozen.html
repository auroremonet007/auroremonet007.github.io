Case study · Entrepreneurship

A drink concept, from idea to Business Model Canvas

Finding a need, studying it and answering it, as a team of three.

Setting
Entrepreneurship course, CNAM, 2025
Team
3 engineering students
My role
Ideation, needs analysis, study of our strengths, planning, part of the Business Model Canvas
Deliverable
Concept note with a Business Model Canvas
Result
Idea approved by the professor

The project

The brief: find a need, study it, then design an answer. We chose rough mornings after a night out. Our answer: Cocozen, a coconut water-based drink to have at the end of the evening, to feel better at the end of the night and the next day.

The approach

  • Analysis of the functional drinks market and of new drinking habits
  • Three personas: a student who goes out often, a young professional who cares about wellbeing, a bar manager looking for a distinctive product
  • Two customer segments: consumers (B2C) and bars, festivals and events (B2B)
  • A complete Business Model Canvas: value proposition, partners, channels, revenue

What I learned

Starting from a real problem rather than a product, and building a value proposition for specific personas: it's the same logic a Product Owner follows.
