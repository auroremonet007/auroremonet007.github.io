About

The need first, with tech close at hand.

My background

I started in multimedia, with a two-year technical degree (DUT) in Multimedia and Internet at the University of Toulon. I then earned an engineering degree in computer science and multimedia at CNAM PACA, through a work-study program at EGERIE, from 2023 to 2026.

At EGERIE, I was a Product Owner on a cyber risk management platform. I ran workshops with enterprise clients, wrote user stories, prioritized the backlog and followed deliveries with developers, QA and design. My engineering thesis is about this work: evolving an existing module under tight resource constraints.

What drives me

I don't want to do pure tech. What I enjoy is supporting teams and bridging the gap between clients and engineers. I put the need first: understanding what users are really trying to achieve, before talking about solutions.

My technical background is a real asset for this. I understand developers' constraints, I can tell when a request impacts the architecture, and I can explain these issues simply to clients.
